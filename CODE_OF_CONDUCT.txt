LogBrew code of conduct

This applies to everyone who takes part in LogBrew repositories, issues, pull
requests, and discussions.

Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good intent, and give
others room to be new at something.

These are not allowed:

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- discrimination of any kind
- publishing someone's private information without their permission
- spam, and repeated off-topic posts after a maintainer asked you to stop

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, and issues that break these
rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

Report a problem to support@logbrew.co. We read every report and keep the
reporter's identity private.
